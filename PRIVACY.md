# Nika Flame WhatsApp Assistant Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This policy explains how Nika Flame handles information when customers contact its WhatsApp Business account and those messages are processed by the Nika Flame WhatsApp Assistant.

## Information we process

The assistant may process:

- WhatsApp account identifiers, including a phone number, username, and business-scoped user ID when WhatsApp supplies them;
- profile information supplied by WhatsApp, such as a display name;
- messages sent to Nika Flame and Nika Flame's replies;
- message timestamps, delivery status, and WhatsApp message identifiers; and
- operational records needed to prevent duplicate sends, apply automation safeguards, and investigate failures.

## How we use information

Nika Flame uses this information to:

- respond to product, order, availability, and other business enquiries;
- show conversations to authorised Nika Flame operators;
- prepare suggested replies and, where enabled, send replies subject to configured safety controls;
- maintain conversation continuity and message-delivery status; and
- secure, troubleshoot, and improve the messaging service.

Nika Flame does not sell information received through this assistant.

## Service providers

WhatsApp and Meta transmit and deliver the messages. When AI-assisted drafting is enabled, relevant conversation text may be sent to OpenAI to prepare a reply. Suggested or automated replies remain subject to the assistant's configured access, allowlist, confidence, and human-review controls.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Storage, access, and retention

Conversation data is stored in Nika Flame's restricted business workspace. Operator access requires authentication and is limited by assigned roles. Webhook requests are verified using Meta signatures, and outbound requests use idempotency controls to reduce duplicate sends.

Operational webhook and audit histories are subject to a 90-day retention setting unless a different period is required for security, dispute handling, legal obligations, or an active customer conversation. Active conversation records may be retained while they are needed to provide support and maintain business records. Backups, when created, are stored in restricted local storage.

## Your choices and requests

You may ask Nika Flame to access, correct, or delete information associated with your WhatsApp conversation, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. Send the request to the Nika Flame WhatsApp Business account at **+61 491 477 942** and state that it is a privacy or data-deletion request.

You can stop using the assistant at any time by not sending further messages or by blocking the Nika Flame WhatsApp Business account in WhatsApp.

## Changes to this policy

Nika Flame may update this policy when the assistant, its providers, or its legal obligations change. The latest version and its update date will remain available at this URL.
